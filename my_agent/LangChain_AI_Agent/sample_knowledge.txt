=== ENGINEERING CAMPUS INFORMATION & RULES 2026 ===

1. ATTENDANCE & ACADEMIC ELIGIBILITY:
   - Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject to be eligible for mid-term and final semester examinations.
   - Medical leaves require submission of an official medical certificate signed by a registered practitioner within 3 days of returning to campus.

2. LABORATORY & RESEARCH FACILITIES:
   - The High-Performance Computing (HPC) Lab is open 24/7 for senior and graduate research students. Access requires card swipe authentication.
   - The Robotics and Embedded Systems Lab is located on the 3rd floor of Block B. Equipment checkout hours are between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM.

3. CAPSTONE PROJECT GUIDELINES:
   - Final year engineering projects must be registered by September 15th.
   - Every team consists of 3 to 4 students and must have an assigned faculty mentor.
   - Interdisciplinary projects combining Computer Science and Electrical Engineering receive priority seed grants up to $1,500.

4. SCHOLARSHIPS AND GRANTS:
   - The Dean's Merit Scholarship covers 50% tuition for students maintaining a Cumulative GPA (CGPA) above 3.8.
   - Innovation Grants are awarded annually by the Incubation Cell for student startups. Application deadline is October 31st.
